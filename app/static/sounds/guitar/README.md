# Guitar samples

The sound of a guitar part (any of General MIDI's guitars, and the ukulele and mandolin, which New
score gives a guitar's sound) in playback, Practice, the fretboard below and exported audio.

They're FreePats' Spanish classical guitar (version 2019-06-18), a nylon-string guitar recorded in
2008 by Roberto of zenvoid.org for the FreePats project, as 44.1 kHz 16-bit mono FLAC. These are 15
of its 48 notes, about every minor third from the low E2 to C6: E2, G2, B2, D3, F3, G♯3, B3, D4,
F4, A4, C5, D♯5, F♯5, A5 and C6. A note between two of them is the nearer one played a little
higher or lower.

- **Taken from:** <https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Guitar/acoustic-guitar.html>, the "SFZ FLAC" download
  `SpanishClassicalGuitar-SFZ+FLAC-20190618.7z`, on 15 November 2026.
- **Unchanged:** the files are the archive's own FLACs, byte for byte, renamed only so a sharp isn't
  a `#` in a web address (its `G#3.flac` is `Gs3.flac` here).
