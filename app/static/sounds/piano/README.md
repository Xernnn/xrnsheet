# Piano samples

The sound of a piano part in playback, Practice, the keys below the score and exported audio.

They're the Salamander Grand Piano, a Yamaha C5 recorded by Alexander Holm: one velocity layer,
one note every minor third from A0 to C8 (A, C, D♯ and F♯ in each octave, 30 notes), as 44.1 kHz
stereo MP3s. A note between two of them is one of them played a semitone higher or lower.

- **Taken from:** <https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/>, the set Tone.js's examples use
  (its repository is <https://github.com/Tonejs/audio>), on 15 November 2026, unchanged.
- **Original release:** Salamander Grand Piano V2 and V3, <https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3>,
  also kept at <https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano>.
