        About xrnsheet

        xrnsheet reads a photo or PDF of sheet music into a score you can edit, play, practise and print.
        It's made for piano, guitar and violin players: Read shows the score large and clean, Write enters notes from the keyboard, fretboard or fingerboard below it, and Practice loops, slows down, counts in, plays one hand alone and waits for you.
        Pages are engraved by Verovio in the Leland music font (SIL Open Font Licence).
