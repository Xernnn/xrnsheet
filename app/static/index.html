      About xrnsheet

      xrnsheet reads a photo or PDF of sheet music into a score you can edit, play and print.
      Its window, shortcuts and page layout follow MuseScore Studio, so what you know from there works here. It is a separate program and shares no code with it.
      Pages are engraved by Verovio in the Leland music font (SIL Open Font Licence).
