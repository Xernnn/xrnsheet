Keyboard shortcuts
NStart or stop entering notes
A to GWrite that note, then move on
Shift+A to GAdd that note to the chord just written
1 to 7Length, from a 64th to a whole note. 5 is a quarter note
.Dotted
0Rest
TTie to the next note
# - =Sharp, flat, natural
↑ ↓Move a note. With Ctrl, move an octave
← →Previous or next note
SpacePlay or stop
Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+YUndo, redo
Del, Ctrl+DelMake a rest, or remove the beat
Ctrl+3Make a triplet from the chosen length, then type its notes. 5 6 7 make other tuplets
Shift+click, Shift+← →Select a stretch of notes
Ctrl+C X V ACopy, cut, paste, select everything on the staff
SSlur a stretch, or this note to the next
< >Crescendo, diminuendo
Shift+S V N OStaccato, accent, tenuto, marcato
/Grace note before this note
RRepeat this note or stretch
JSwap for the same note under another name
XFlip the stem
Ctrl+Alt+1 to 4Write in that voice, or move the selection to it
EnterStart a new line at this bar
Drag a selected noteMove it up or down
Click a dynamic, slur or textChange or remove it
Ctrl+LType lyrics. Space moves on, - joins syllables, ↓ ↑ change verse, Enter finishes
Click the staffWhile entering notes, place a note there. Shift+click adds it to a chord
